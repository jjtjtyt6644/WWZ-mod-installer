Includes everything from v1.0.3 to v1.0.8.

- **Cheats page:** infinite ammo, health, class equipment and any other number, applied live while you play. No need to close the game.
- **Quick cheats (F5–F8):** type the number you see, press its key, use it, press again. A rising beep means it's locked on. **F9** switches every cheat on or off.
- **Class detection:** WWZ Mod reads your class from your save, so F7 follows it (Slasher: stun gun charges, Exterminator: Molotovs, and so on).
- **Make permanent:** learn the game code that lowers your ammo or equipment once. After that it switches on by itself every match and after restarts, with no scanning. Only the game's own code is changed, and you can turn it off or delete it any time.
- **Advanced scanner:** find any value yourself by hex address (whole numbers or decimals, exact, decreased, increased, changed or unchanged), then freeze it.
- **In-game overlay (F10):** a small click-through panel over the game that shows which cheats are on. Works in Borderless or Windowed mode.
- **Keep WWZ Mod on top** option, and faster background drawing.

**Cheats are for solo/offline play or private lobbies with friends who agree. Don't use them in public matches.**
