### Fixes

- **The in-game overlay no longer disappears behind World War Z.** When the game fully opened and took over the
  screen, its window could end up on top of the overlay. The overlay now puts itself back on top about once a second,
  without taking focus or clicks away from the game.
- **Exclusive fullscreen warning.** In exclusive Fullscreen, Windows lets nothing draw over the game. WWZ Mod now
  tells you once per game session when that's the case: in WWZ go to **Options → Video → Display mode** and pick
  **Borderless** (it looks the same, and the overlay shows).

Cheats are for solo/offline play or private lobbies with friends who agree. Don't use them in public matches.
